📐 Forensic Calibration Standards

The Science of 55/45 Centering & Subgrades

The difference between a PSA 9 ($600) and a PSA 10 ($4,850) is often a 0.2mm shift in border balance. Understanding the four technical subgrades prevents heartbreaking grade disappointments.

1. Centering Tolerance Standard Front: 55/45 • Back: 75/25

PSA allows up to 55/45 or 60/40 on the front for a Gem Mint 10. Beckett requires 50/50 exact symmetry on all four sides for a Pristine 10 Centering subgrade.

2. Corner Fiber Integrity Zero Whitening under 20x

Modern chromium stock features fragile foil edges. Any micro-ding or paper fiber fraying visible under 20x stereomicroscopy instantly drops the grade to a 9.

3. Surface Print Lines & Dimples 100% Raking Light Audit

Factory roller marks, wax residues, and micro-dimples are scrutinized under angled raking light. Our conservators safely remove surface oils before encapsulation.

4. Laser-Cut Edge Sharpness Flawless Foil Retention

Edges must exhibit clean mechanical cuts free of chipping, foil slivering, or rough burrs along the borders.

How to Safely Pack Raw Cards for Submission

More than 15% of raw cards submitted by collectors lose grades in transit due to improper packing. Follow our strict 4-step preservation protocol.

1

Soft Penny Sleeve Insertion

Slide the raw card into an acid-free soft penny sleeve. Pro Tip: Carefully snip a 1mm diagonal cut on the top-right corner of the sleeve to prevent the card's bottom corner from catching or fraying upon entry.

✓ Prevents corner dings
2

Semi-Rigid Card Saver 1

Place the sleeved card into a semi-rigid holder (Card Saver 1). Never use rigid plastic top-loaders with tape; grading technicians at PSA cut Card Savers open to prevent card damage during extraction.

✓ PSA & BGS mandatory standard
3

Team Bag & Cardboard Sandwich

Bundle up to 10 Card Savers into a resealable poly team bag. Sandwich the bundle between two rigid pieces of corrugated cardboard that extend at least 1 inch past the edges. Secure with blue painter's tape.

✓ Crush & bend protection
4

Double Box with Bubble Wrap

Place the protected sandwich inside an inner box surrounded by 2 inches of dense bubble wrap, then seal that inside an outer shipping box. Never use padded envelopes for high-value cards.

✓ Shock & impact insulation
