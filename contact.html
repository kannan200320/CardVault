Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, definitive answers regarding private viewings, vault intake, authentication, and wire escrow settlements.

In-person viewings take place inside Suite 800 at 9400 Wilshire Boulevard, Beverly Hills. Appointments must be requested at least 24 hours in advance via our concierge intake form above or phone desk (+1 800 592-CARD). Qualified collectors are escorted into a biometric private viewing salon equipped with Leica stereomicroscopes and UV raking lights to personally inspect multi-thousand and million-dollar slabs under armed security supervision.
Every slab transacted through CardVault undergoes acoustic sonic weld resonance testing to confirm the acrylic casing has never been breached, pried, or resealed. Furthermore, each certification number is verified in real-time against PSA, Beckett (BGS), and SGC central registries, backed by our unconditional 100% lifetime buyback authenticity guarantee underwritten by Lloyd's of London.
When you purchase a slab and allocate it directly into CardVault depository custody, ownership title transfers digitally within our insured Beverly Hills facility without triggering state sales taxes. You avoid all shipping transit risks, save up to 10.25% in local sales taxes, and can re-list or liquidate the card peer-to-peer at any time with instant cash settlements.
All outbound physical dispatches travel via FedEx Priority Overnight with required adult ID signature verification. High-value slabs are packed in water-resistant, crush-tested Pelican™ armor cases with tamper-evident security tape, active cellular GPS tracking beacons, and comprehensive transit insurance up to $1,000,000 per parcel.
CardVault accepts institutional Fedwire bank transfers, ACH clearinghouse payments, major credit cards (via 256-bit SSL encrypted gateway), and approved stablecoin escrow (USDC). For transactions exceeding $10,000, our dedicated wire desk provides real-time bank coordinate verification and immediate release of title upon funds settlement.
