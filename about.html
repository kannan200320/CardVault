Fiduciary Standards

Our Binding Code of Collector Ethics

The sports collectibles industry has long suffered from opaque auctions, trimmed cards, and phantom bids. CardVault operates under a legally binding charter of collector ethics that governs every employee, curator, and partner.

✓

Strict Anti-Shill & Bid Integrity

No employee, board member, or affiliated broker is permitted to participate in CardVault auctions under any circumstances. All bid logs undergo automated cryptographic hashing to prevent simulated demand or reserve manipulation.

✓

50x Microscopic Flaw Disclosure

We never conceal print dots, corner fiber softening, roller marks, or hairline refractor scratches. Our 50x optical zoom audits document both the virtues and imperfections of each slab before any card is cataloged.

✓

Segregated Client Escrow Accounts

Buyer settlement funds are held in isolated FDIC-insured escrow trusts until 48 hours following physical delivery or vault title confirmation. Consignor holdings are legally held in bailment and are immune to corporate liabilities.

✓

Ultrasonic Sonic Weld Verification

Every encapsulated card is cross-checked against PSA, Beckett, and SGC digital population registries. We utilize refraction spectroscopy to verify that the acrylic sonic weld has not been cracked, buffed, or swapped with a counterfeit.

✓

Independent Valuation Comp Anchors

CardVault valuations are never inflated. Every estimated price range is calculated directly from public auction sales across Heritage, Goldin, and PWCC with complete serial-number matching and date verification.

✓

100% Lifetime Buyback Guarantee

If an encapsulated card sold on CardVault is ever proven to be recolored, altered, or misattributed by an accredited third-party grader, we issue an immediate 100% refund with full shipping and grading cost indemnification.
